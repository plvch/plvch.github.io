Personal Finance Foundations

Most working people already own an asset worth hundreds of thousands of euros — their future earnings. "Personal Finance Foundations" is a live group course on personal finance in Germany, built for immigrants, that teaches how to see that asset, protect it, and convert it into financial capital. It teaches how to think about money; the reference numbers and mechanics live in the free wiki, geldchen.com.

Five modules of two hours each, and every module ends with a personal number you compute yourself on a one-page worksheet:

  • You are an asset — human capital, present value, gross vs. net, inflation
  • Floor, ceiling, and the gap — spending as structure, not willpower; the savings rate as the one lever you own
  • Big decisions — the true full cost of housing, cars, and paid education, before signing
  • The conversion ladder — five rungs in strict order, from emergency fund to automated diversified investing
  • The dark side — insurance through one filter, the pension system as an asset you already own, and a catalog of red flags

Format: small group, live — online or in person in Berlin, in Russian/English with German terms taught as vocabulary. The first cohort is in preparation. If it sounds relevant, write me which format and language would fit you — you'll get the dates and conditions as they firm up.

Scope. The course is financial education, run under the geldchen brand. No product recommendations, no advice on specific instruments, insurance, taxes, or legal questions — where personal advice is needed, the course names the licensed profession to go to. It is separate from the plvch planning practice.
